Factory reset

Warning — this action is irreversible.
Erasing NVS will reset all settings, tamagotchi progress, LED preferences, eye calibration, and the operating mode to the factory defaults.

After the reset, the badge will reboot into SleepMode. The Bluetooth server will be disabled, and the badge will be unreachable.

How to enable Bluetooth

1
Make sure the badge is in awake mode (eyes open).
2
Hold CENTER for 0.5 s to open the menu.
3
Navigate to Settings with LEFT / RIGHT, confirm with CENTER.
4
Go to Bluetooth → Server and press CENTER to toggle it ON.
5
Come back here and click Connect to badge. A 4-digit PIN will appear on the badge screens,confirm it by pressing CENTER on the badge.
